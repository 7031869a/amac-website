Why no practice score will be marked pass or fail

Practice here reports a raw score and a sub-domain breakdown. It will not show a verdict, a predicted scaled score, or a percentage compared against the pass mark, because no such conversion exists.

What you can do instead

Untimed random practice works today against whatever is in the bank. It makes no claim about exam conditions and reports no pass mark.
