Don't just read the model answer.

The same station, played three ways — with the reasoning behind each verdict, not just the words. A candidate who lists every risk perfectly can still fail. A candidate who names a limit honestly can still pass. This is why.

Consent — Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy

✗ Fail Listed fourteen risks in a fixed order. Never asked what the patient already understood. Invited questions after the decision had effectively been made.
✓ Safe Pass Covered procedure, risks, and alternatives including doing nothing. Checked understanding by asking the patient to repeat it back.
★ Distinction Established what mattered to this patient before weighting the risks. Named conversion to open surgery as a safety decision, not a failure. Declined to invent a risk percentage.
