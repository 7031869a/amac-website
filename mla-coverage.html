How this works

Practised means you have answered at least one question on it. Secure means you have answered at least a fifth of its questions (never fewer than three, or all of them if there are fewer) and got 80% or more right.

Each AKT question is linked to the one condition and one presentation it mainly tests, so a symptom that only appears in passing is not counted. Where a GMC entry is a broad heading (such as antepartum haemorrhage), it is covered through the more specific entries listed under it. Questions that test something the GMC list does not name are not counted towards any entry. Coverage shows how widely you have practised, not whether you are ready for the exam.

Your progress comes from the AKT bank in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and clearing your browser data resets it.
