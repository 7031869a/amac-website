How this circuit runs
Before each station you have 90 seconds to move to the room and read the instruction sheet. Each station lasts 8 minutes. You will get a two-minute warning, and the station ends when time is up. As in the exam, there is no countdown on screen and you see only the instruction sheet. Start each station by confirming your name and GMC number. You mark yourself after the whole circuit, not during it.

How the real PLAB 2 pass works
The examiner marks each station in three domains (data gathering, technical and assessment skills; clinical management; interpersonal skills) and also gives a grade: unsatisfactory, borderline, satisfactory or good. The grades are used after the exam to set each station's pass mark. To pass, you need both: a total at or above the overall pass mark (the sum of the station pass scores plus one standard error of measurement), and a minimum number of stations passed. That minimum is calculated for each exam; in 2024–25 it was 9 or 10 of 16. AMaC cannot reproduce the real station pass marks, so use the domain totals and the number of stations you graded satisfactory or better as a guide only.
