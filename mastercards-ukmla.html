AMaC Master Cards

105 Master Cards, organised by CPSA station type and clinical skill. Each card shows only the sections it genuinely has, so the number of sections varies from card to card.

The CPSA asks one question, in station after station: "On the first Monday of Foundation Year 1, could this person be left alone with this patient for eight minutes and keep them safe?"

It is not a checklist row that says "professional". It is the global rating — the examiner's answer to "would I want this person on my ward?" — and it is influenced by everything you do from the knock on the door to the thank-you.

DISTINCTION — Purpose Detect danger early · communicate risk honestly · escalate to the right person in time · leave a record.

Examiner lens: "Would I trust this doctor with my patient?" Safety is the domain in which a single omission — a missed red flag, an unsafe discharge — can fail an otherwise good station.

Outcomes for Graduates: Professional skills — diagnosis and medical management; use of clinical reasoning.

Outcomes for Graduates: Professional values — self-awareness, managing personal wellbeing, recognising the effect of stress on performance.
