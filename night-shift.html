THE FOUNDATION DOCTOR · NIGHT SHIFT

Practise a night on call before you do one.

A practice night in six decisions. Bleeps keep arriving and waiting patients change: decide what to do with each bleep and who to see next, then see what happened to every patient by morning.

DRAFT · Not yet signed off by a senior reviewer.
Read this first. Night Shift is for practice before or between shifts, not for use during one. It is a teaching model with fixed time points and outcomes written by doctors in advance; it is not a simulation of real timings. How to manage each patient is on the linked Bleep Card. Your trust's escalation policy and the senior on duty always take priority over this exercise.
