# Live questions affected by NICE guideline changes — proposed corrections for approval

**Scope:** both live banks (AKT 10,959 · PLAB 1 3,131) scanned for content touched by three NICE changes, each confirmed directly on nice.org.uk on 8 Oct 2026:

1. **Sepsis:** NG51 withdrawn and replaced by NG253 (aged 16+, published Nov 2025, updated Sept 2026), NG254 (under 16s) and NG255 (pregnancy). Adults: NEWS2-based risk; balanced crystalloid; 250 mL boluses up to 1,000 mL.
2. **Upper GI cancer (NG12, May 2025):** dysphagia, or 55+ with weight loss and upper abdominal pain/reflux/dyspepsia → *suspected cancer pathway referral* (no longer "urgent direct-access endoscopy within 2 weeks").
3. **Gynaecological cancer (NG12, April 2026):** ovarian — age-specific CA125 thresholds (40–49 ≥35; 50–59 ≥31; 60–69 ≥24; 70–79 ≥25; 80+ ≥31) and no CA125 in isolation under 40; endometrial — post-menopausal bleeding criteria now say "cannot be attributed to HRT".

**Result:** 174 questions flagged by the scan and read individually. **147 need no change.** **27 need correction** (22 clinically outdated, 5 wording only). Every proposed fix was then checked by an independent reviewer (21 accepted as written, 6 amended — the amendments are already included below). **No keyed answer changes.** Two questions (Q902, N9859) have their correct option reworded; same letter.

One correction is not a guideline change: **Q540 and its PLAB 1 version RES007 state a NEWS2 of 9, but the observations in the stem add up to 13.** The fix corrects the score and adds the missing oxygen saturation so it can be calculated.

## Ovarian / CA125 (NG12, April 2026)

### AKT CR035 — outdated

**Why:** Keyed diagnosis is unaffected, but the pearl states the old single 'CA-125 ≥35 → ultrasound' rule; NG12 (April 2026) uses age-specific thresholds (31 IU/ml for a 52-year-old) and no CA-125-in-isolation under 40.

**pearl — before:** NICE NG12: in women (especially aged 50 or over) with persistent or frequent (particularly more than 12 times a month) bloating, early satiety, pelvic/abdominal pain or urinary urgency/frequency, measure CA-125. **pearl — after:** NICE NG12 (updated 2026): in women aged 40 or over (especially 50 or over) with persistent or frequent (particularly more than 12 times a month) bloating, early satiety, pelvic/abdominal pain or urinary urgency/frequency, measure CA-125 and arrange an urgent direct-access ultrasound of the abdomen and pelvis if it meets the age-specific threshold (35 IU/ml at 40–49, 31 at 50–59, 24 at 60–69, 25 at 70–79, 31 at 80+); refer on the suspected cancer pathway if the scan suggests ovarian cancer. Under 40, do not use CA-125 in isolation — consider urgent ultrasound. Refer directly if there is ascites and/or a pelvic or abdominal mass (not obviously fibroids). CA-125 is not diagnostic alone — false positives occur in endometriosis, PID, pregnancy and liver disease.
